# apps/mock-business-app — the business console, disconnected

> Read root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) first. Package rules only.

- **It is a MOCK, and that is a licence, not an apology.** It renders the business console against fixtures so the screens can be LOOKED at: `web-business` is OTP-gated, its shell calls an Edge Function before it paints a row, and the live catalog is empty. Local only — `pnpm dev` on **:3006**, no Vercel project, no CI deploy, no domain.
- **No backend, and no dependency that could become one.** No `@supabase/*`, no `fetch`, no server action, no auth. `next.config.ts` allows one image host. If a change here needs a network call, the change is wrong.
- **Everything is a client component under one external store** (`mock/store.ts`, read through `mock/MockStore.tsx`'s `useMock`). `useSyncExternalStore`, never `useState` seeded in an effect. Screens that would 404 on a place the stored scenario DOES hold must wait for `hydrated`.
- **`mock/scenario.ts` is the query planner; `mock/fixtures.ts` is the database.** A page NEVER imports fixtures for a place's own switches — `resolveWorld` applies the scenario's overrides, and reading round it renders a place the panel cannot move. `MOCK_NOW` is fixed: a fixture on the wall clock makes every visual diff noise.
- **`MockPanel` is the point of the app.** It reaches the states nobody can reach on purpose: the four `RailMode` shapes, the five Stripe rungs, viewer, unpartnered, and every list empty. Keep it OUTSIDE the console's chrome — a panel welded into the rail is a ninth row nobody can unsee.
- **The MOCK strip is not decoration.** Every name, number and photo here is invented and must stay invented; the strip is what stops a screenshot being read as production. Never add a real place, a real person, or a real phone number.
- **The law it mirrors is `web-business`'s** — one rail from `RAIL_ROWS`, one row shape, rows never dim, Soon lives on the page, hidden ≠ protected (`PlaceTabGate` 404s), the canonical address names its place and the flat twin resolves onto it, a view reads nothing because the layout published `PlaceScope`, Account is one full-width card, Credits has no grand total. When that law moves, this app may lag. **Drift is not a bug here** — `lib/` and `globals.css` are SNAPSHOTS, owned by nothing, checked by no gate. Re-snapshot by hand; never wire a generator to them.
