# apps/mock-business-app — the business console, disconnected

> Read root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) first. Package rules only.

- **It is a MOCK, and that is a licence, not an apology.** It renders the business console against fixtures so the screens can be LOOKED at: `web-business` is OTP-gated, its shell calls an Edge Function before it paints a row, and the live catalog is empty. **Deployed** (MESITA-1903): its own Vercel project, canzeco team, Root `apps/mock-business-app`, skip-unaffected by `ignoreCommand`, **no `regions`** — it talks to nothing; no hop to pin. No env vars, no domain. Locally `pnpm dev` on **:3006**.
- **No backend, and no dependency that could become one.** No `@supabase/*`, no `fetch`, no server action, no auth. `next.config.ts` allows one image host. If a change here needs a network call, the change is wrong.
- **Everything is a client component under one external store** (`mock/store.ts`, read through `mock/MockStore.tsx`'s `useMock`). `useSyncExternalStore`, never `useState` seeded in an effect. Screens that would 404 on a place the stored scenario DOES hold must wait for `hydrated`.
- **`mock/scenario.ts` is the query planner; `mock/fixtures.ts` is the database; `mock/atlas.ts` is the Atlas vocabulary.** A page NEVER imports fixtures for a place's own switches — `resolveWorld` applies the scenario's overrides, and reading round it renders a place the panel cannot move. `MOCK_NOW` is fixed: a fixture on the wall clock makes every visual diff noise.
- **`MockPanel` is the point of the app.** It reaches the states nobody can reach on purpose: the four `RailMode` shapes, the five Stripe rungs, viewer, unpartnered, every list empty. Keep it OUTSIDE the chrome — welded in, it is a fifth destination nobody can unsee.
- **The MOCK strip is the only thing between this and a stranger now.** It is publicly reachable, so the strip and `robots: noindex, nofollow` are load-bearing, not garnish. Every name, number and photo is invented and must stay invented: never a real place, person, or phone number.
- **The law it mirrors is `web-business`'s** — one menu from `NAV_ROWS`, one tab shape, tabs never dim, Soon lives on the page, hidden ≠ protected (`PlaceTabGate` 404s), the canonical address names its place and the flat twin resolves onto it, a view reads nothing because of `PlaceScope`, Settings is one full-width card, Credits has no grand total. **The IA no longer mirrors it (MESITA-1975):** four tabs on one top line — Place, Setup, Activity, Settings — no rail, no drawer, no venue in the chrome; the Place page names it. **Drift is not a bug here** — `lib/`, `globals.css`, `components/brand/`, `components/admin-ui/` and `components/place-manage/` are SNAPSHOTS, owned by nothing, checked by no gate. Re-snapshot by hand; never wire a generator. **This app LEADS** — the IA lands here first.
