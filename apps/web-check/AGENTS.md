# apps/web-check — Mesita Check, the staff app (check.mesita.ai)

- **The staff side of Tickets v2/v3 (MESITA-806/813/814/849/850):** the waiter scans the QR a guest generated in their consumer app and lands on `/<code>` (`check.mesita.ai/<code>`) — proof the ticket is real, plus the visit's staff actions: enter the bill (optional unless the place requires it) and mark paid / close the ticket. Story and Google review rows are **informational only** (MESITA-849) — the guest completed them before scan; staff never approve or reject. Root page = explainer + hand-typed code fallback. Legacy `/check/<code>` redirects to `/<code>`.
- **Proof screenshots don't change the staff job** (MESITA-1030): story/review submissions now carry a screenshot in the `ticket-proofs` bucket, but they still self-verify, and `shapeCheckPayload` only ships `story.screenshot_url` for pre-v3 tickets still awaiting a verdict. Reading a proof image is **Ojo**'s job (admin `/ojo-config`, staged — engine MESITA-1034), never the floor's. Don't grow an approve/reject affordance here.
- **The name is "Check", never "Checkout" (MESITA-815).** `checkout` in this repo means **Stripe** (`stripe.checkout`, `checkout_url`) — the subscription billing flow. `check` is this page's own shipped vocabulary: the `check-web-*` EFs, the `check_code` column, `CHECK_URL_BASE`, and the `check-web` caller in the closed set (Notion Product Rules §A). It also says both jobs at once — *check* = la cuenta, *check* = verify. Don't reintroduce "checkout" here: it implies Mesita processes payments, which the page itself denies.
