# apps/web-business — business console (business.mesita.ai)

> Read root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) first. Package rules only.

- **Deploys** via Vercel `mesita-web-business`, Root `apps/web-business`; `mesita-web-business-legacy` is a decoy. Light theme, semantic tokens.
- **Routes** (`lib/console-routes.ts`): **the address names its subject; ORG_PAGES = ORG_TARGETS = ORG_RAIL_TARGETS, ONE array.** `/places/<id>/{profile,menus,reviews,capabilities,rewards,admin}` · `/orgs/<id>/{configuration,places(/new),customers,payments,activity}` · `/account` · `/orgs/new`. **Bare `/orgs/<id>` forwards** (307 → `/configuration`) and alone reads Stripe's `?connect=` (mint links with `orgRootHref`). Most names have a **flat** twin, ONE file `(shell)/[flat]`: 307 onto the canonical address for the remembered scope; nothing selected renders `NoPlaceYet`; unknown 404. **`places` has none**; no flat name may collide with a static dir. The rail links CANONICAL. `/orgs/<id>/switch?to=` writes the org cookie and clears the place cookie. `/` is 307, never 308. **No `next.config.ts` rule may shadow a live address.**
- **Nav:** LEFT RAIL, **no wordmark**, **THREE SECTIONS**: Account (a link, no subitems), then an **Organization selector** and a **Place selector**, each over its pages (Admin super-admin only); Collapse in the footer. **ONE row shape** — a selector IS a row (same height, same glyph box), ranked by colour not size; pages **indent under their selector**, one depth, **hairline seams** between groups; no eyebrow; one pill, no id. **Ceremonies and metas live in the selector menus**, never a row; selectors guard first. At `w-16`: chips only. Rows never dim; **Soon lives on the PAGE**. `Sidebar.tsx` is DARK — `--sidebar-*` only, page tokens in `RailSelector.tsx`. `AppShell.tsx` resolves scope into `RailScopeContext`; `main` is the only scroller.
- **Add place is a SEARCH**: one bar, one list; each row states its Mesita state and verb (Create · Claim · Open). A place another org holds gets none.
- **One box, said once.** `shared/Section.tsx` IS the box: name in the heading, fact in the body, action in `right` — never a description or label repeating the title. **Configuration is five boxes**: Brand · Members · Stripe · Partnership · Developers — not Places, which has a row. **No door rows. Account is the person alone.** Never two badges for one fact.
- **Capabilities and Rewards are ONE engine, two displays.** `PromosSection` takes a `zone`; `ZONE_ROWS` (`sections/controls/offerings.ts`) owns the split, proven by test.
- **Never rebuild ticket UI**: Validate is staff-only; leave `business-web-*-ticket*`.
- **Place heading, not a bar.** `PlaceHeading.tsx` is the `h1`. The rail learns a pool place from `OpenPlace.tsx` and unsaved edits from `PlaceNavBridge.tsx`, the only `PlaceContext` reader.
- **Layout:** fluid, no max-width. `(shell)/layout.tsx` sets `SHELL_GUTTER`, the rail outside it; `SHELL_BLEED` goes full-bleed. Pages are fragments with `loading.tsx`; forms cap at `FORM_COLUMN_CLASS`; save = `PlaceSaveBar`.
- Plan SKUs: **Free** + **Verified** (`plan=pro`). **"Membership" banned.** Roles: owner/editor/viewer.
- CI: `web-business.yml` — lint · typecheck · test · build, path-filtered. Clients call `business-web-*` EFs.
